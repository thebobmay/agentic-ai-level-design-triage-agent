# AI Level Design Triage Report

## Design Brief
Reduce the frustration in this level but preserve the core layout. Do not redesign it.

## Interpreted Intent
- Target audience: general
- Difficulty target: medium
- Novelty preference: medium
- Desired feel: less frustrating
- Hard constraints: preserve the core layout, do not redesign the level
- Soft preferences: reduce frustration
- Detected conflicts: (none)

## Triage Decision
- Action: recommend_revision
- Playtest readiness: revise_before_playtest
- Confidence: high
- Rounds: 1

## Diagnosis
The candidate is structurally valid, matches the medium difficulty target, and has high novelty. However, there is a localized difficulty spike at column 9 where a gap is combined with an enemy, which can create a frustrating moment. This conflicts with the intent to reduce frustration and provide a less frustrating experience for a general audience. The rest of the pacing is balanced, and the level opens with a safe zone, but this spike is a concern for the desired feel.

## Tradeoff Reasoning
- The level is structurally sound and does not need redesign, respecting the hard constraints.
- Difficulty and novelty are on target for the brief.
- The detected spike (gap plus enemy) is likely to frustrate players, which is at odds with the explicit intent to reduce frustration.

## Revision Prescription
- Separate the enemy from the gap at column 9 by moving the enemy at least 2 columns away from the gap, or relocating the gap so it does not coincide with the enemy. (This edit will reduce the frustration caused by the layered hazard, aligning the level with the intent to be less frustrating while preserving the core layout.)

Playtest question: Does the revised segment feel less frustrating at the former spike location, and does it maintain a medium challenge overall?

## Independent Critic
- Verdict: approve
The Director's recommendation is sound. The diagnosis correctly identifies that, while the level is structurally valid and matches the medium difficulty and novelty targets, the presence of a layered hazard (gap plus enemy) at column 9 creates a localized spike in difficulty. This spike is inconsistent with the explicit intent to reduce frustration for a general audience. The prescribed revision—separating the enemy from the gap—directly addresses this issue without violating the hard constraints (preserving the core layout and not redesigning the level). No other issues are present, and the recommendation does not overreach or underreach. The action is appropriate and well-justified.
