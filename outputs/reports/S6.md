# AI Level Design Triage Report

## Design Brief
Reduce the frustration in this level but preserve the core layout. Do not redesign it.

## Interpreted Intent
- Target audience: general
- Difficulty target: medium
- Novelty preference: medium
- Desired feel: less frustrating
- Hard constraints: preserve the core layout, do not redesign the level
- Soft preferences: reduce frustration
- Detected conflicts: (none)

## Triage Decision
- Action: recommend_revision
- Playtest readiness: revise_before_playtest
- Confidence: high
- Rounds: 1

## Diagnosis
The candidate is structurally valid, matches the medium difficulty target, and has high novelty. However, there is a localized difficulty spike where a gap is combined with an enemy near column 9. This spike could cause frustration, which conflicts with the intent to reduce frustration and provide a less frustrating experience for a general audience. The rest of the pacing is balanced, and the level opens with a safe zone, but this spike is a concern for the desired feel.

## Tradeoff Reasoning
- The level is structurally sound and does not need redesign, so the core layout should be preserved.
- The overall difficulty and pacing are appropriate for a medium segment, but the spike at column 9 is inconsistent with the intent to reduce frustration.
- Addressing the spike will better align the level with the desired feel without violating hard constraints.

## Revision Prescription
- Separate the enemy from the gap near column 9 by moving the enemy at least 2-3 columns away from the gap, or reposition the gap so it does not coincide with the enemy. (This reduces the localized difficulty spike, making the segment less frustrating while preserving the core layout and overall challenge.)

Playtest question: Does the revised segment feel less frustrating at the gap/enemy section, and does it maintain an appropriate medium challenge overall?

## Independent Critic
- Verdict: approve
The Director's recommendation is sound. The diagnosis correctly identifies a localized difficulty spike where a gap is combined with an enemy, which could cause frustration and conflicts with the intent to reduce frustration for a general audience. The prescription to separate the enemy from the gap is appropriate, respects the hard constraint to preserve the core layout, and does not overreach. All other aspects of the level (difficulty, novelty, pacing, and safe zone) are consistent with the brief. The recommendation does not violate any hard constraints and is well-justified.
