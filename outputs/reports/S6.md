# AI Level Design Triage Report

## Design Brief
Reduce the frustration in this level but preserve the core layout. Do not redesign it.

## Interpreted Intent
- Target audience: general
- Difficulty target: medium
- Novelty preference: medium
- Desired feel: less frustrating
- Hard constraints: preserve the core layout, do not redesign the level
- Soft preferences: reduce frustration
- Detected conflicts: (none)

## Triage Decision
- Action: recommend_revision
- Playtest readiness: revise_before_playtest
- Confidence: high
- Rounds: 1

## Diagnosis
The candidate is structurally valid, matches the medium difficulty target, and has high novelty. However, there is a localized difficulty spike where a gap is combined with an enemy near column 9. This spike could cause frustration, which conflicts with the intent to reduce frustration and provide a less frustrating experience for a general audience. The rest of the pacing is balanced, and the level opens with a safe zone, but this spike is a concern for the desired feel.

## Tradeoff Reasoning
- The level is structurally sound and does not violate any hard constraints.
- Difficulty and pacing are appropriate for a medium segment, except for the localized spike.
- The detected spike (gap plus enemy) is likely to frustrate players, which is at odds with the intent to reduce frustration.

## Revision Prescription
- Separate the enemy from the gap near column 9 by moving the enemy at least 2-3 columns away from the gap, or relocating the gap so it does not coincide with the enemy. (This will reduce the frustration caused by the layered hazard, aligning the segment with the intent for a less frustrating, medium-difficulty experience.)

Playtest question: After revision, does the segment still feel fair and less frustrating at the former spike location?

## Independent Critic
- Verdict: approve
The Director's recommendation is sound. The diagnosis correctly identifies a localized difficulty spike where a gap is combined with an enemy, which could cause frustration and conflicts with the intent to reduce frustration for a general audience. The rest of the level is structurally valid, matches the medium difficulty target, and maintains high novelty. The prescribed revision—separating the enemy from the gap—directly addresses the only significant issue without violating any hard constraints. No further issues are present, and the recommendation is appropriate for the brief.
