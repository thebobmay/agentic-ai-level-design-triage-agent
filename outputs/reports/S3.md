# AI Level Design Triage Report

## Design Brief
I want this to be easy and welcoming for brand new players, but also pack in lots of enemies and big gaps so it feels intense and hardcore.

## Interpreted Intent
- Target audience: beginner
- Difficulty target: easy
- Novelty preference: medium
- Desired feel: welcoming, intense, hardcore
- Hard constraints: (none)
- Soft preferences: lots of enemies, big gaps
- Detected conflicts: Beginner accessibility and easy difficulty conflict with the request for lots of enemies, big gaps, and an intense, hardcore feel.

## Triage Decision
- Action: request_clarification
- Playtest readiness: not_ready
- Confidence: high
- Rounds: 1

## Diagnosis
The interpreted intent contains a detected conflict: the brief requests a beginner-friendly, easy segment that is also intense and hardcore, with lots of enemies and big gaps. These goals are at odds, as intensity, many enemies, and large gaps typically raise difficulty and reduce accessibility for beginners. The candidate level is structurally valid and features a safe opening, but it contains a localized difficulty spike (a gap combined with an enemy) that is not suitable for beginners. The intent conflict must be resolved before further triage.

## Tradeoff Reasoning
- Beginner accessibility and easy difficulty require low challenge, gentle pacing, and high leniency, but the brief also requests intensity, lots of enemies, and big gaps, which are hallmarks of higher difficulty and lower leniency.
- The candidate level reflects this conflict: it is valid and has a safe opening, but includes a difficulty spike that would frustrate beginners, and its overall difficulty is medium, not easy.

## Independent Critic
- Verdict: approve
The Director correctly identifies a fundamental conflict in the design intent: the brief requests both beginner accessibility/easy difficulty and intense, hardcore elements with lots of enemies and big gaps, which are inherently at odds. The candidate level is structurally valid and includes a safe opening, but features a localized difficulty spike (gap plus enemy) and overall medium difficulty, which do not align with the easy/beginner target. The recommendation to request clarification before further triage is appropriate, as proceeding without resolving the intent conflict would not yield a sound design. No hard constraints are violated, and the diagnosis is consistent with the facts and intent. The action does not overreach or underreach.
