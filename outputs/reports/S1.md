# AI Level Design Triage Report

## Design Brief
I want a beginner friendly first level that still feels a little exciting. Keep the opening approachable for brand new players.

## Interpreted Intent
- Target audience: beginner
- Difficulty target: easy
- Novelty preference: medium
- Desired feel: exciting, approachable
- Hard constraints: keep the opening approachable for brand new players
- Soft preferences: beginner friendly, first level
- Detected conflicts: (none)

## Triage Decision
- Action: recommend_revision
- Playtest readiness: revise_before_playtest
- Confidence: high
- Rounds: 1

## Diagnosis
The level is structurally valid and matches the easy difficulty target, but it lacks an opening safe zone: the first enemy appears at column 3, which is too soon for a beginner-friendly, approachable first level. This conflicts with the hard constraint to keep the opening approachable for brand new players. Pacing is otherwise balanced, and novelty is within the desired medium range.

## Tradeoff Reasoning
- The level is valid and easy, with no difficulty spikes, which fits the brief for a beginner-friendly first level.
- However, the absence of a safe opening stretch means new players encounter a challenge almost immediately, which can feel abrupt and unwelcoming for the intended audience.
- Adding a short safe zone at the start will better serve the intent without compromising the rest of the design.

## Revision Prescription
- Move the first enemy further right so that the first 5 columns are free of hazards or enemies. (This creates a safe opening stretch, giving new players time to orient before facing a challenge, which aligns with the approachable, beginner-friendly intent.)

Playtest question: Does the new opening feel welcoming and give players enough time to get comfortable before the first challenge?

## Independent Critic
- Verdict: approve
The Director's recommendation is sound. The diagnosis correctly identifies that the absence of a safe opening zone conflicts with the hard constraint for a beginner-friendly, approachable first level. The prescription to move the first enemy further right to create a safe stretch is appropriate and directly addresses the issue without overreaching. All other aspects of the level (difficulty, novelty, pacing, structural validity) are consistent with the intent and constraints. No additional issues or constraint violations are present.
