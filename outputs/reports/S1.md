# AI Level Design Triage Report

## Design Brief
I want a beginner friendly first level that still feels a little exciting. Keep the opening approachable for brand new players.

## Interpreted Intent
- Target audience: beginner
- Difficulty target: easy
- Novelty preference: medium
- Desired feel: exciting, approachable
- Hard constraints: (none)
- Soft preferences: beginner friendly, exciting feel, approachable opening
- Detected conflicts: (none)

## Triage Decision
- Action: recommend_revision
- Playtest readiness: revise_before_playtest
- Confidence: high
- Rounds: 1

## Diagnosis
The level is structurally valid and matches the easy difficulty target, but it lacks an opening safe zone for beginners. The first enemy appears at column 3, which is too abrupt for the intended approachable, beginner-friendly experience. The pacing is front-loaded, with all challenge at the start and none in the rest of the segment, which weakens the sense of excitement and does not provide a balanced or engaging experience for new players.

## Tradeoff Reasoning
- The level is valid and easy, which fits the brief, but the absence of an opening safe zone is a significant issue for beginners.
- The challenge is concentrated at the very start, which can feel abrupt and does not allow players to orient themselves before encountering an enemy.
- The rest of the level is empty, missing opportunities for excitement or engagement, and does not deliver on the 'exciting' feel requested.

## Revision Prescription
- Move the first enemy further right, so that the first 5 columns are free of hazards or enemies, creating a safe zone at the opening. (This gives beginners time to orient and matches the approachable, beginner-friendly intent.)
- Add a few simple, spaced-out coins or gentle platforming elements after the safe zone to maintain engagement and excitement without raising difficulty. (This provides a sense of progression and excitement while keeping the level easy and approachable.)

Playtest question: Does the new opening feel welcoming and give beginners enough time to get oriented before the first challenge?

## Independent Critic
- Verdict: approve
The Director's recommendation is sound. The diagnosis correctly identifies that the absence of an opening safe zone is a significant issue for a beginner, easy segment, as the first enemy appears too early (column 3), which can be abrupt and unwelcoming. The recommendation to move the enemy further right and add simple collectibles or gentle platforming after the safe zone is appropriate and directly addresses the intent for an approachable and exciting feel. The level is structurally valid and easy, but the pacing and opening are not suitable for the target audience without revision. No hard constraints are violated, and the prescribed edits are reasonable and targeted. The recommendation does not overreach or underreach.
