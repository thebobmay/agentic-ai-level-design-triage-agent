# AI Level Design Triage Report

## Design Brief
I want a beginner friendly first level that still feels a little exciting. Keep the opening approachable for brand new players.

## Interpreted Intent
- Target audience: beginner
- Difficulty target: easy
- Novelty preference: medium
- Desired feel: exciting, approachable
- Hard constraints: keep the opening approachable for brand new players
- Soft preferences: beginner friendly, first level
- Detected conflicts: (none)

## Triage Decision
- Action: recommend_revision
- Playtest readiness: revise_before_playtest
- Confidence: high
- Rounds: 1

## Diagnosis
The level is structurally valid and matches the easy difficulty target, but it lacks an opening safe zone: the first enemy appears at column 3, which is too soon for a beginner-friendly, approachable first level. This conflicts with the hard constraint to keep the opening approachable for brand new players. Pacing is otherwise balanced, and novelty is within the desired medium range.

## Tradeoff Reasoning
- The absence of an opening safe zone is a significant issue for a beginner audience, as it does not give new players time to orient before encountering a challenge.
- The rest of the level is simple and approachable, and the overall difficulty is appropriate for the target audience.
- Novelty is within the desired range, so no changes are needed there.

## Revision Prescription
- Move the first enemy further right, so that the first 5-6 columns are free of hazards or enemies. (This creates a safe opening stretch, giving new players time to orient and making the level more approachable as a first experience.)
- Optionally, add a visual cue such as a coin in the opening columns to guide the player forward. (This enhances approachability and provides gentle guidance for beginners.)

Playtest question: After revision, do new players feel comfortable and oriented before encountering the first challenge?

## Independent Critic
- Verdict: approve
The Director's recommendation is sound. The diagnosis correctly identifies that the absence of an opening safe zone conflicts with the hard constraint for a beginner-friendly, approachable level. The prescription to move the first enemy further right and optionally add a guiding coin is appropriate and directly addresses the issue. All other aspects (difficulty, novelty, pacing, structural validity) are consistent with the intent and constraints. No overreach or underreach is present; the recommendation is targeted and justified.
