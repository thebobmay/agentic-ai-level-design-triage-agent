## Diagnosis

However, the measured novelty is extremely low (0.986 similarity to a known level), indicating it is highly derivative. The difficulty is easier than the medium target, but this is not a hard constraint and may be acceptable for a draft. Pacing is balanced, and there is a proper safe zone. No difficulty spikes are present.

## Tradeoff Reasoning
- The candidate is structurally sound and safe for playtest.
- The low novelty matches the brief's preference for established style, but the similarity is so high that it should be flagged as derivative for tracking.
- Difficulty is easier than targeted, but not in conflict with the brief's soft preferences.

## Playtest Questions
- Does the segment feel too familiar or repetitive compared to existing levels?
- Is the challenge level appropriate for a medium segment, or does it feel too easy?

## Independent Critic
- Verdict: approve
The Director's recommendation is sound. The candidate level is structurally valid, matches the intent for low novelty and established style, and contains no hard constraint violations. The extremely high similarity to a known level is appropriately flagged as a derivative draft, which is the correct action given the brief's low novelty preference. There are no remaining issues that would prevent playtest.
